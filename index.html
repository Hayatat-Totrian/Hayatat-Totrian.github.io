Tomoki Hayata is a Ph.D. student at the Graduate School of Business Administration, Kobe University. His research focuses on addressing managerial challenges through quantitative methods and data analysis, providing rigorous evidence to support better corporate decision-making. In particular, he studies the role of marketing in achieving sustainable competitive advantage and business strategy.
