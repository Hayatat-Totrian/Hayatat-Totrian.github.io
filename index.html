Studying how firms allocate resources and how markets respond—and why neither can be understood without the other.

Tomoki Hayata is a Ph.D. candidate in business administration at Kobe University. His research sits between strategy, which asks how firms allocate their resources, and marketing, which asks how customers respond to what firms offer and say. He treats the two as one problem: an allocation creates value only when the market responds to it, and that response shows where resources should go next. His evidence comes from panel data on Japanese firms and text from online platforms, complemented by formal models.
