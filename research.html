Do Higher-Order Resources Generate Sustained Competitive Advantage for Firms? A Replication Study of Wibbens (2019)
高次の資源は企業に持続的な競争優位をもたらすか？：Wibbens（2019）の追試

Tomoki Hayata and Toru Ogasawara

Kaikei Kagaku（会計科学）, 2023, e2022(6): 1–6. (in Japanese)

PDF

Abstract

A replication study of Wibbens (2019, Strategic Management Journal), which examines whether higher-order resources contribute to the persistence of firm performance. Using financial data on all listed Japanese firms (fiscal years ending March 2000 through March 2021; 49,962 firm-years), the ARMA(2,2) estimates closely mirror those of the original study, indicating that higher-order resources also shape performance persistence among Japanese firms and that conventional AR(1) models may underestimate the duration of competitive advantage.
